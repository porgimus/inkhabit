# Planning the vegetable bed

Soil: what everything else depends on
  - dig in compost before anything is sown
  - clay holds water, sand lets it go
> test a handful after rain

Sowing: when each crop goes in
  - peas and broad beans: early, they shrug off cold
  - tomatoes: only after the last frost
    a late frost undoes a month of work

! Rotate the beds: never the same crop two years running
  pests and diseases build up where their food stays put

Watering vs feeding
  - water the roots, not the leaves
  - feed little and often once the fruit has set
